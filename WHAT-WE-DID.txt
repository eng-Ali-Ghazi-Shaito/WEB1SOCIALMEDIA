PART 4: QUESTIONS YOUR PROFESSOR MAY ASK
==============================================================

Q: Why validate in the database if Zod validates too?
A: Zod only protects requests that pass through the API. CHECK and UNIQUE
   protect against bugs, race conditions and raw SQL.

Q: Why user_a_id < user_b_id?
A: It stores each pair in one fixed order, so (A,B) and (B,A) are the same row,
   and the unique index really allows one conversation per pair.

Q: Why are the FOLLOW notification indexes partial?
A: post_id is NULL for FOLLOW, and NULLs never conflict in a normal unique index.
   The partial index on (recipient_id, actor_id) WHERE type = 'FOLLOW' fixes that.

Q: Why a trigram index for search?
A: ILIKE '%ali%' cannot use a normal B-tree index. pg_trgm splits text into
   3-letter pieces, and a GIN index maps each piece to its rows.

Q: What is the shadow database?
A: A temporary database that migrate dev creates, replays all migrations into,
   then drops. It detects drift without touching your data.

Q: Why does prisma7.config.ts still read process.env?
A: It is the one exception to the env.ts rule. Importing env.ts there would make
   `prisma generate` require the JWT secrets at build time.

Q: What is a known weakness?
A: btrim only strips spaces, so a message of only tabs or newlines passes the
   CHECK. Zod's .trim() must catch that in the API layer.
